# What's New in Detour 0.4.0

- **Quick Detour** — press Option-Command-N in any app to look something up in a small window of its own, without touching your spaces. Open in moves the page into a tab of any space when it turns out to be worth keeping. Option-Command-click a link to open it in one, and an unused Quick Detour closes itself after a while.
- **Link Routes** — send links to the space they belong to: by site, by address or by the app they came from, for links from other apps, typed addresses and links clicked in a page. Set them up in Settings > Links.
- **Quarantine** — a route, or the default for links from other apps, can open a link in a throwaway Quick Detour that sees none of your cookies or site data, records no history and strips tracking parameters. Open in loads the page fresh in a space when you decide to trust it.
- **Site search** — type a site's keyword then Tab or Space, or press Tab when the address completes to a site, to search that site from the command palette. Sites that advertise their search are added on their own; Settings > Search edits the list.
- **Dev mode** — pages on localhost and other local development addresses get a hazard-striped sidebar row and a toolbar with buttons for the Web Inspector's Console and Network, the element picker, and a picture of the visible area or the whole page. Turn it on or off for any site from the address bar.
- **Sidebar** — hover a tab for a card with its full title, address and a picture of the page. Media playing in a tab you have left gets a now-playing bar at the bottom of the sidebar. Peek badges on tabs and split panes show their own card.
- **Peek and splits** — Open in Split View from a Peek; a split started from a favorite or pinned tab opens in the normal section.
- **Picture in picture** — automatic picture in picture on tab switch can be turned off per site, from the site settings popover.
- **New icon** — Detour's icon is now a DETOUR road sign with a bent arrow.
- **Settings** — panes size to their contents and lists scroll on their own; Quick Detour, Links and Search have panes of their own.
- **Command palette** — typing part of a host completes to the site, Backspace keeps working after moving off a completion, the list no longer flickers while typing, and every suggestion comes from the palette's own profile.
- **Content blocker** — element-hiding exceptions no longer switch off network blocking on a site, more filter rules apply, block lists compile without freezing the window, and a Private window's per-site switch is forgotten when it closes.
- **Extensions** — notifications, history and management work, behind their permissions and scoped to their profile. Native helpers such as 1Password's never outlive Detour, and one that stops reading no longer freezes the app. Popups open at their content's size, and windows and tabs an extension opens land in its own space.
- **Reliability** — a link to an IPv6 address no longer crashes Detour, a failed page load names and retries the address that failed, a tab keeps its title when its web process dies, and a tab opened only for a download closes again.
