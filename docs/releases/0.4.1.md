# What's New in Detour 0.4.1

- **Private windows** — closing the last Private window clears its cookies, sign-ins and site data right away, instead of keeping them until Detour quits, and extensions allowed in Private start the next Private session fresh.
- **Peek** — File > Open Peek in New Tab (Command-O) keeps the page you are peeking at as a tab. Undo or Reopen Closed Tab brings back a Peek you closed. Settings > General can turn Peek off for pinned tabs and favorites; Shift-click still peeks. Pages you view in a Peek now appear in History.
- **Quick Detour** — Open Link in Quick Detour in a link's menu, also inside a Peek, and Open in Quick Detour in a sidebar tab's menu. The Dock menu hides or shows all Quick Detour windows at once.
- **Link Routes** — a From app route also applies to a link that starts Detour. If Detour can't tell which app that link came from, it opens in Quarantine wherever a From app route to Quarantine could have matched it. Choosing an app and leaving the URL empty makes a route for every link from that app, without switching to Any URL first.
- **Tab archiving** — a tab playing sound is never archived, and its countdown starts again when the sound stops.
- **Recent tabs** — hold Control and press W in the Control-Tab switcher to close the highlighted tab.
- **Address bar** — Copy Link and Site Settings always sit at the right end, with extension icons to their left, in the sidebar and in Quick Detour.
- **Help menu** — links to the feature guide, keyboard shortcuts, switching from Arc, release notes and reporting an issue.
- **Command palette** — the empty-window hint no longer shows through behind the palette.
