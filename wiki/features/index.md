# Components

# Features

* [Command palette](command-palette.md) - The overlay used for URL entry, tab switching, history and web search: its two modes, how it is opened and dismissed, inline completion, keyboard handling, and how focus returns to the page.
* [Content blocking](content-blocking.md) - The built-in blocker: EasyList-family filter lists compiled to WKContentRuleLists, per-profile list toggles, the per-site switch implemented through a WebKit preferences SPI, and the blocked-resource counter.
* [Dev mode](dev-mode.md) - Which URLs count as local development (localhost, *.localhost, 127.0.0.0/8 in any IPv4 spelling, [::1], 0.0.0.0, *.test over http/https), the per-site override and its popover switch, the one function the effective state goes through, how a dev-mode tab's sidebar row, split half and favourite tile are marked with a host line and, on the window's selected row only (like Arc), a hazard border, and the striped toolbar above a dev-mode page (inside the tab's container, frame-based so the docked Web Inspector keeps working) with its capture buttons (visible area and full page, to the clipboard or Downloads) and its Console, Network and element picker buttons.
* [Downloads](downloads.md) - How a navigation becomes a download, what DownloadManager tracks and persists, how a Private window's downloads are kept apart and cancelled when it closes, the sidebar popover and badge, the fly-to-button animation, and the CRX path that reuses the list.
* [Error pages](error-pages.md) - What a failed load looks like: the browser-error:// page, which failures earn one (only those before commit) and which are ignored, how the tab keeps the URL the user asked for, and how retry, restore and background tabs behave.
* [External application links](external-apps.md) - What happens when a page navigates to a non-web scheme such as zoommtg: or mailto: - the confirmation sheet, the pure launch policy, and the per-profile 'Always allow' store keyed by origin and scheme.
* [Find in page](find-in-page.md) - The Cmd+F find bar: a per-window FindBarView driving WKWebView.find on the selected tab, with a match counter computed separately in JavaScript.
* [History page](history-page.md) - The detour://history/ page: a profile-scoped list of visits in an ordinary tab, with search, a time-range filter, selection and delete, and a natively confirmed Clear History.
* [Link routing (Air Traffic Control)](link-routing.md) - Air Traffic Control's model (routes with a URL condition, a source-app condition or both, a target, a sort order; one Default target), the pure resolver and every rule it applies (first match wins, domainIs on a dot boundary with www, ports, IDN and IP literals defined, source-app routes external-only, one-way into Private, Quarantine and Quick Detour external-only, the Default for every origin; Quick Detour a visible target since TASK-193.5, opening a new Quick Detour on the front window's profile, never Private), storage in migration v20, why a space a route targets cannot be deleted (also from undo and redo), links from other apps (the pure placement rule, reading the sender from the Get URL Apple Event, delivering test links without reaching the production app), and typed URLs and links clicked in a page: the pure eligibility rule (new-tab opens route, plain clicks only to another host, never redirects, scripts, popups, Option/Shift-clicks, Peeks, subframes; host not site), the WebKit signals that tell a click from a script or a redirect, a route winning over the pinned/favourite Peek intercept,, Cmd+L never routing, and the Settings Links pane (TASK-191.3): one row per route (URL, From app, Open in), Any URL and Any App as the 'no condition' choices, drafts and refused edits kept on their rows while the store holds the last valid version, validation in the store (empty or invalid pattern, no condition, a missing or Private space; an unchanged broken target stays editable), broken routes shown, the Default with its caption, live space and store updates, and the blocked-deletion alert's Open Links Settings… button. *(draft)*
* [Now-playing bar](now-playing-bar.md) - The media bar at the bottom of the sidebar for audio playing in a tab the user has left, in any space (modelled on Arc): favicon, Previous, Play/Pause, Next and Mute, expanding on hover to the title, artist, artwork and a dismiss button; the pure rule that decides which tab it shows, how each window evaluates it, and what the controls do. *(draft)*
* [Quick Detour](quick-detour.md) - A small window with one page for a quick lookup, opened from a Spotlight-like palette panel (File > New Quick Detour Window, or a system-wide hotkey, Opt-Cmd-N by default, from any app): the panel, the global hotkey (Carbon, no permission; what conflicts it can and cannot detect; activating with only the new window forward; the Settings control), the tab in no space, history under a per-profile scope labelled on the History page, Private, extensions seeing a popup window, what is not restored, and Open in <Space> (Cmd+O): the default space, the dropdown grouped by profile, the same tab and web view handed to a space of the same profile, a reload in another profile's, Private into a Private window, and the teardown order; and the profile pill (TASK-193.4): its menu, switching reloading the address in a new page on the other profile in the same window (a close and an open for extensions, the new profile's history scope, into and out of Private with the download question), and Open in following; and TASK-193.5's other entry points and archive: Opt-Cmd-click on a link (any frame, in a Peek, a Private page's into Private; never routed) or on a sidebar tab (a copy of its address: normal, sleeping, a split row's clicked pane, pinned, favourite), links from other apps through Air Traffic Control's Quick Detour target (the frontmost window's profile, never Private), the archive (closed-tab rows under the profile's Quick Detour scope, no migration; closed, at quit, and auto-closed; restore reopens a window with the back/forward list; Private never; profile deletion), the idle auto-close (a pure wall-clock rule, what counts as use, a periodic and wake check, a Debug override) and the Settings controls; and TASK-210's bar: the main window's site settings popover in place of the extensions list, the host and Open in dragging the window once a press moves (a first press on a background window only brings it forward), no subtitle on the dropdown, and the palette's own prompt; and TASK-213's popover layout: the host as its header, picture in picture and dev mode in a section of their own, left out of a Quick Detour's. *(draft)*
* [Tab hover card](tab-hover-card.md) - The card shown beside the sidebar when the pointer rests on a tab row or favourite tile: the full title, the URL with its host emphasised and a page thumbnail; when it shows, what it describes, where thumbnails come from (memory, then a per-row JPEG on disk) and what dismisses it.
