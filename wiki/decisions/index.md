# Decisions

* [A split is two adjacent tabs sharing a group id, two panes at most](two-pane-splits.md) - Why a split tab is modelled as a shared splitGroupID on two ordinary tabs rather than a container object or a partner pointer, and why it is limited to two side-by-side panes.
* [Closed tabs are read from the database only, with no row cap](closed-tabs-database-only.md) - Why the closed-tab stack has no in-memory mirror and no launch load, why nothing evicts records by count, and what was rejected on the way.
* [Extension updates must pass CRX3 verification](crx-verification-gate.md) - Why an extension update is installed only if its CRX3 signatures verify and its signing key derives the installed id, why a manifest with a key is classified as unpacked, and what the first-install path still does not check.
* [Extensions are off in Private unless allowed](private-extensions-opt-in.md) - Why extensions do not load in the Private profile until the user allows each one, why that was implemented as a changed default rather than a migration, and what was rejected.
* [History search matches titles only through the scope's own visits](history-own-title-matching.md) - Why a page title may match or label a history result only through visits made in the asking scope, how the title index makes that cheap, and the designs that were tried and dropped on the way.
* [ID-based drag payloads and pure drop resolvers](id-based-drag-payloads.md) - Sidebar drag pasteboards carry stable IDs resolved at drop time, and every drop decision is a pure function over enums, replacing row-number payloads handled inline in the view controller.
* [Observer protocol for the store, Combine for the tab](observer-protocol-over-combine.md) - TabStore announces structural changes through the TabStoreObserver protocol, while each BrowserTab publishes its own state with @Published.
* [Run extensions on WebKit's WKWebExtension and fill the gaps with polyfills](native-webextension-with-polyfills.md) - Detour replaced its own extension runtime with WebKit's native WKWebExtension and a thin polyfill layer; what that bought, the working rules it led to, and what it costs.
* [Single web view owner](single-webview-owner.md) - When two windows show the same tab, one window hosts the live web view and the others show a snapshot, with hosting following window focus.
* [Splits survive pinning, and a pinned split lives on the entries](splits-survive-pinning.md) - Why pinning a whole split keeps it a split, and why a pinned split's group id is stored on the two PinnedEntry objects while their backing tabs carry none.
* [The polyfill patches members and never replaces chrome or browser](never-replace-chrome-globals.md) - Replacing the chrome/browser globals with a Proxy silently disconnected service workers from runtime messaging; the polyfill therefore patches members in place, and anything that cannot be patched is handled natively.
* [Two databases](two-databases.md) - Session state and browsing history live in separate SQLite files, browser.db and history.db.
